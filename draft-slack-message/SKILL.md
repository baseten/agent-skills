## Task

Draft a Slack message about: $ARGUMENTS

## Guidelines

### For Updates/Announcements

- Lead with the key info (what changed, what's new)
- Keep it scannable - use bullets for multiple points
- Include relevant links/PRs
- End with next steps or asks if needed
- Emojis welcome for visual breaks and tone

### For Team Chat

- Conversational and friendly
- Direct - get to the point quickly
- Humor/personality encouraged
- Match the energy of the channel

### For Asking Questions

- Context first (brief)
- The actual question (clear)
- What you've tried (if relevant)

## Tone

- Informal but professional
- Direct without being terse
- Personable - you're talking to teammates
- Confident but not arrogant

## Output

Provide the draft message ready to paste into Slack.
